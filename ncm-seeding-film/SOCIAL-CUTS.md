## 45-second cut — 9:16 vertical

- **Film:** https://d2ol7oe51mr4n9.cloudfront.net/user_3GDYTMzP23BD6MHwIHFhhk07Vl5/f36198a0-b5e7-4001-9f7b-7e9b44aaa66b.mp4
- 1080×1920, 24 fps, 45.0 s, silent.

Full 16:9 frame centred on a vertical canvas over a blurred, darkened fill of itself. Nothing is
cropped out — every composition stays intact, which matters here because the aircraft frequently
sits at frame edge.

**Why not AI reframe:** `reframe` would outpaint genuine new content into the vertical margins
rather than filling them, but it costs 419 credits at 1080p and 216 at 720p. The ffmpeg treatment
is free. If the budget ever allows, AI reframe is the better result.

## Known gaps

- **Both cuts are silent.** For feed distribution this is the single biggest handicap — a music
  bed would do more for performance than any re-render.
- The aircraft carries the **v1 livery**, not the green NCM scheme, so the cuts stay internally
  consistent with the Thunder master.
