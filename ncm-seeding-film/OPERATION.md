## Deleted

**Photorealistic, removed for style consistency:** CFO Abu Dhabi countdown, ground service loading,
runway HUD telemetry, SEEDING ARMED panel, photoreal cockpit flare-fire, seeding beneath the storm,
rain landing.

**`CFO_LADY` — removed.** 720×1280 vertical and a different character-render style; pillarboxing
made the mismatch worse rather than better. The decision and order beats are covered by the
cel-shaded Thunder forecast-office and radio shots instead.

## Upgrades over the previous cut

- `hangar-ncm` replaces a rendered apron taxi — correct green livery, correct signage.
- `flares-firing` replaces a rendered rack shot — two-row rack, rearward burn, smoke trails
  streaming back over the mountains. The best single shot in the film.

## Honest notes

- **Silent.** The largest remaining gap at 92 s.
- **Livery is not uniform.** Shots 02, 03, 07, 10, 14, 15 come from earlier Thunder renders and do
  not carry the green NCM scheme. Style is consistent; livery is not. Fixing it means
  reference-locked re-renders of those six at ~10 credits each.
- **The rain payoff is gone.** The only landing-under-rain footage was photorealistic. The film now
  ends on the aircraft at rest rather than on the weather it made.
