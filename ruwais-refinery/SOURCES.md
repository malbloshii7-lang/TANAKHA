## Representative figures (derived, not published)

- **CDU cut yields per crude** (`scenarios.json → cdu_cuts_pct`): approximate
  volume distributions consistent with the public assays above (Murban: light,
  ~31% atmospheric residue; Upper Zakum: ~44%; Basrah blend: ~52%). The two
  assay PDFs could not be fetched from inside the build sandbox (network
  egress blocked), so cut points were rounded from public assay summaries —
  treat as illustrative.
- **Product yield slates** (`yields_pct`): estimates constructed so that
  conversion-unit capacities (RFCC 127k, HCU 57k, ARD 177k) and the cut yields
  above roughly balance. ADNOC publishes no per-crude yields for Ruwais.
- **Feed sulfur t/d**: arithmetic — barrels × density(API) × sulfur wt%. The
  formula is displayed in the UI next to the number.
- **Utilization %, hydrogen demand %**: qualitative estimates to convey
  direction and magnitude, not measurements.
- **Flow volumes** (`flows.json`): representative kb/d used for pipe thickness
  and particle density; they approximate but do not close a mass balance.
- **All 3D positions/sizes**: schematic layout, deliberately not geographic.
