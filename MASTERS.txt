MEASURED (on the encoded files; QC.md has every figure)
- Exact frame counts, 50 fps, BT.709 colour tags and audio matched to picture in every file.
- Every loop is one stream with one keyframe. Its last frame runs into its first like two neighbouring frames: a mean
  difference of 0.007 grey levels or less, with at least 99.2% of pixels unchanged. Loop W is a still frame: 0.0001.
- The films are encoded at CRF 18 with a keyframe every 5 s. Each keyframe re-codes the paper grain: 0.86 to 1.08 in
  still passages. The cuts are that size, and below the floor between two separate encodes (part 1 -> part 2):
    part 1 -> loop W 0.95, loop W -> part 2 1.06 (floor 1.20);
    fallback part 1 -> its loop W 0.94, its loop W -> part 2 1.14 (floor 1.24);
    part 2 -> loop A 0.53.
- Part 2 -> loop B: 4.57, by design. The title gives way to the dedication. A 0.5 s dissolve on the media server
  softens it if a hard change is not wanted.
- Loudness: part 1 -16.4 LUFS, part 2 -14.0, loop W -20.0, loops A/B/C -24.0.
- True peak: part 2's AAC reaches -0.8 dBTP (its WAV -1.1), part 1's -1.3. The recorded score should leave 1.5 dB
  of headroom so that the encoded files also stay under -1 dBTP.

PICTURE SAFETY
No full-frame flashes, no full-frame red, no strobing. The twentieth drop's gold glint ramps over 0.6 s. The
photosensitivity pre-check (ITU-R BT.1702 model) passes on every picture: no flash, the largest concurrent change 4.4%
of the screen. It is not the certified test: run the Harding (ITU-R BT.1702) test on the final master before sign-off.
