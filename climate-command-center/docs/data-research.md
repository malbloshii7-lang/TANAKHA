## Black Swan Risk Assessment Summary

### Climate Finance Tail Risks
1. **Concentration Risk**: 76% of renewable energy consumption concentrated in IDA/least-developed countries — structural dependency on climate finance flows
2. **Geographic Asymmetry**: North America emits 13.66t CO2/capita vs Sub-Saharan Africa at 0.70t — 19.5x disparity creates political fragility in climate negotiations
3. **Energy Transition Gap**: High-income countries use 4,178 kg oil eq/capita vs Kenya at 539 kg — equity tensions threaten COP consensus
4. **Small Island States**: Pacific island states at 1.53t CO2/capita but disproportionate climate vulnerability — existential risk concentration

### Risk Alerts
- **Yellow Watch**: Climate finance承诺 gaps vs actual disbursements (consistent ~$100Bn shortfall annually)
- **Orange Caution**: Correlation between high-emitting regions and climate finance donor fatigue
- **Black Swan Scenario**: Simultaneous withdrawal of multiple donor nations from climate commitments would cascade through IDA countries' renewable infrastructure investments

### Historical Analogues
- 2008 financial crisis: Climate finance dropped 23% in aftermath
- 2020 COVID: Temporary 8% emissions drop but followed by 5.7% rebound
- Current portfolio vulnerability concentrated in single-point-of-failure: USD-denominated climate finance
