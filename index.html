Sub: Independence Day Greetings from the President of WMO
Dear Dr. Echeverry Priet,

It is with immense pleasure that the World Meteorological Organization extends its warmest congratulations to the Republic of Colombia on the auspicious occasion of its Independence Day on July 20th. This significant day marks not only a pivotal moment in Colombia's rich history but also celebrates the nation's enduring spirit, resilience, and remarkable journey towards progress and prosperity.

Colombia's national meteorological service, IDEAM, has demonstrated commendable advancements in strengthening hydrometeorological monitoring, early warning systems, and climate services. A notable achievement includes the development and release of the 2018 Atlas of Climate, Radiation, and Wind of Colombia, which serves as a crucial tool for renewable energy development, risk management, and climate-smart decision-making, showcasing a significant technological transformation and providing valuable insights for climate adaptation and resilience. Furthermore, Colombia has been actively enhancing its water-monitoring systems and strengthening wildfire preparedness, particularly in response to the challenges posed by El Niño events.

We also commend Colombia's proactive engagement in climate resilience initiatives, such as spearheading the First World Virtual High Mountain Summit in 2020, which brought together global stakeholders to discuss biodiversity, ecosystem services, and climate variability. The leadership of Ms. Gheidy Gallo Santos, with her extensive experience in public policy and international cooperation, has been instrumental in driving forward initiatives that contribute to national development and environmental sustainability.

The World Meteorological Organization deeply values its partnership with Colombia and remains steadfast in its shared commitment to addressing the pressing challenges of climate change. We extend our heartfelt wishes for Colombia's continued success, peace, and prosperity in the years to come.

Yours sincerely,
