# Sources

Every fact on screen and in the subtitles is listed here with the public source it comes from, or with how it was
computed. The film was checked on 2 October 2026.

The President's office supplied internal documents, used for orientation only:
- the itinerary for Kyrgyzstan;
- the pre-visit briefings for Tonga and Bucharest;
- the Pacific mission outcomes report.

Nothing from them is on screen unless a public source reports it too. Their logistics never appear: flights, hotels,
transfers and security.

## Title and honorific

- **His title.** H.E. Dr Abdulla Ahmed Al Mandous is President of the World Meteorological Organization, elected at the
  19th World Meteorological Congress in June 2023. Sources: his LinkedIn profile (linkedin.com/in/almandoos);
  wmopresident.com.
- **The honorific.** The film keeps «معالي» / H.E. because he has held ministerial rank since the federal decree of
  15 April 2026 (WAM; see the TANAKHA repository's CLAUDE.md). BMKG's report of 23 September 2026 also styles him
  "H.E." and "Minister to Presidential Court".

## Kyrgyzstan (WMO Region II)

- **Bishkek, 8 September 2026.** He held talks with H.E. Kanatbek Chynybaev, Minister of Emergency Situations. They
  covered capacity building, the WMO Regional Training Centre in the UAE, early warnings for sand and dust storms,
  glacier monitoring and hydrological forecasting.
  - Source: his LinkedIn post of 8 September,
    https://www.linkedin.com/posts/almandoos_earlywarningsforall-wmo-kyrgyzstan-activity-7503064318012223488-z3Rk
- **Cholpon-Ata, 9 September 2026.** The 37th session of the CIS Interstate Council for Hydrometeorology took place. The
  WMO President gave one of the welcoming addresses.
  - Sources: the CIS Executive Committee, https://e-cis.info/cooperation/3213/138553/ and
    https://eccis.org/en/news/33119; Belhydromet,
    https://belgidromet.by/en/news-en/view/belhydromet-delegation-at-the-37th-session-of-the-interstate-council-for-hydrometeorology-of-the-14044-2026
  - Kazhydromet and Roshydromet give Bishkek as the place. The CIS Executive Committee, Belhydromet and the itinerary
    give Cholpon-Ata, which the film follows.
- **Cholpon-Ata, 10–11 September 2026.** The International Jubilee Conference marked the 100th anniversary of
  Kyrgyzhydromet.
  - The service was founded on 18 July 1926 as a meteorological bureau of the Kirghiz ASSR.
  - It runs a lake observatory at Cholpon-Ata with research vessels.
  - Sources: Belhydromet, 10 September,
    https://belgidromet.by/en/news-en/view/a-new-day-new-achievements-day-2-of-the-belarusian-delegations-work-in-the-kyrgyz-republic-14053-2026;

## Computed

- **The distance.** The great-circle legs between the airports add up to 43,696 km, shown as "at least 43,700 km".
  - The legs are Dubai–Bishkek–Dubai, Dubai–Pacific–Dubai and Dubai–Bucharest–Dubai. The office confirmed that he
    returned to Dubai between the missions.
  - The Pacific loop's order is not public, so it is taken in the order that makes the total smallest:
    Dubai–Nukuʻalofa–Wellington–Melbourne–Jakarta–Dubai. Jakarta's date (23 September) places it last.
  - The method is the haversine formula on a sphere of radius 6,371.0 km, with these airports: DXB, FRU, TBU, WLG,
    MEL, CGK, OTP.
  - The Equator is 40,075 km.
- **Local times.** These were the standard or summer times on the dates of the visits:
  - Kyrgyzstan UTC+6;
  - Tonga UTC+13;
  - New Zealand UTC+12 (its summer time began on 27 September);
  - Victoria UTC+10 (its summer time began on 4 October);
  - Jakarta UTC+7;
  - Romania UTC+3 (summer time, until 25 October).
- **Early Warnings for All.** This is the UN Secretary-General's initiative that everyone on Earth be protected by early
  warning systems by the end of 2027.

## The plates

### Issyk-Kul (`scenes/kyrgyz.js`, `data/kyrgyz.js`)

- **Viewpoint.** The eye stands at 42.6412 N, 77.1100 E, on the bank behind the beach east of the Cholpon-Ata cape.
  It is 4.5 m above the lake, looking 172° true, with a 20° horizontal field.
- **Skyline.** It was traced from the AWS Terrain Tiles (terrarium, z12, SRTM-derived; registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles)
  over the curved Earth with refraction k = 0.13. The Teskey Ala-Too stands 73–82 km away and 1.4–1.9° up, with summits
  of 3,934–4,557 m.
- **Snow and land colour.** From Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A (tile 43TFG, 11 and 13 September 2026, via the AWS
  sentinel-cogs bucket; "contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2026"):
  - snow is NDSI > 0.4;
  - the snowline is about 4,000 m on north faces, which agrees with the published 3,900–4,000 m (open.kg, "Terskey
    Alatoo Ridge").
- **Sun.** For 09:30 local on 9 September: azimuth 115.8°, altitude 32.4° (NOAA solar position algorithm).
- **Wind and cloud.** From the GFS 0.25° forecast of 9 September 00 UTC (f003):
  - wind 1–3 m/s from the east-north-east, which drives the cat's paws;
  - mid-level cloud over the crest, which supports the cloud bank. Its form and base are illustrative.
- **The observatory.** Kyrgyzhydromet's lake observatory at Cholpon-Ata, with research vessels, is listed in its
  presentation at wecoop.eu (2021) and by Belhydromet. The launch is generic, drawn to scale, with a Kelvin wake.

### Nukuʻalofa (`scenes/tonga.js`, `data/tonga.js`)

- **Viewpoint.** The eye stands on the sea wall west of the harbour, at 21.1268 S, 175.2087 W, 5 m above mean sea level.
  It looks 074° true, at 10:52 local on 16 September 2026, the minute Sentinel-2C imaged the lagoon.
- **Reef flat, islets and cloud.** From Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A, tile 1KFS (19 August 2025 and 16 September 2026,
  sentinel-cogs; "contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025, 2026"):
  - the reef flat and the islets: Pangaimotu, Makahaʻa, Fafa and the Monūafe reef;
  - the day's one cumulus cluster, from the morning's image, its height from its shadow.
- **Islet names.** From GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia.
- **Trade wind.** From the GFS analyses of 15 September 18 UTC and 16 September 00 UTC: 7 m/s from 129°.
- **The canoe.** A pōpao, Tonga's small outrigger canoe, after Tongan Wikipedia "Pōpao" and Te Rangi Hīroa, *Samoan
  Material Culture* (1930). It is illustrative; none was observed that morning.
- **Palm and sea.** The palm in the foreground is generic. The waves breaking on the near reef edge are the trade-wind
  sea; the ocean swell breaks on the outer reefs, below the horizon.

### Region V (`scenes/regionv.js`, `data/regionv.js`)

- **Pressure.** From the NOAA GFS 0.25° analyses (public domain), every 6 hours from 15 September 00 UTC to 23 September
  18 UTC, 2026: 36 analyses.
  - Only each file's PRMSL message was read, at
    `noaa-gfs-bdp-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/gfs.YYYYMMDD/HH/atmos/gfs.tHHz.pgrb2.0p25.anl`.
  - Decoded with ecCodes, smoothed, sampled at 1°, and contoured every 4 hPa.
  - Highs and lows sit at the analysed centres, each with its own central pressure.
- **Coasts.** From Natural Earth 1:50m (public domain).
- **Relief and depth.** From the AWS Terrain Tiles (z5).
- **Members.** The land of WMO Region V's 24 Members is washed in one colour (WMO Community, "About RA V"). The chart is
  the GFS analysis, not the Bureau's or MetService's own.

### Bucharest (`scenes/bucharest.js`)

- **The station.** The observation garden of the Bucharest-Băneasa station (WMO 15420) lies inside the National
  Meteorological Administration's grounds at Șos. București-Ploiești 97. Sources:
  - meteoromania.ro (contact, and the history page: ANM in its present seat since 1961);
  - OpenStreetMap (© OSM contributors, ODbL): node 742024813, inside way 862523834.
- **The garden.** Drawn to the Romanian and WMO platform standard: screens with their sensors 1.8–2.0 m up and the
  doors to the north, a 10 m wind mast. Its actual layout at Băneasa is not public, and ANM's buildings lie behind the
  eye and are not drawn.
- **The weather.** From the station's own report for 1 October 2026, 06 UTC, through OGIMET:
  `AAXX 01061 15420 04970 03602 10118 20053 30192 40304 52006`.
  - No cloud, 20 km visibility, wind north at 2 m/s, 11.8 °C, 1030.4 hPa.
  - It agrees with LRBS 010600Z 35007KT CAVOK.
- **Sun.** Altitude 17.6°, azimuth 113.2°.
- **The observer.** Walks to the screen at the synoptic hour, reads it and writes the register. He is illustrative:
  the 06 UTC report is coded as from an automatic station, and ANM keeps classic instruments too.
