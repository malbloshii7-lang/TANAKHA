# EMPOWER — WMO Election Campaign Film
## Production Script & Animated-Slide Storyboard

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | 4K UHD (3840×2160), 16:9, 25 fps · animated slide sequence (also playable as a continuous film) |
| **Runtime** | ~120 seconds (10 slides ≈ 12 s each; timing per slide below) |
| **Look** | Ultra-photorealistic. Natural volumetric light, shallow depth of field, cinematic grade (soft teal–amber LUT, lifted blacks). No cartoon/CG stylization. |
| **Motion language** | Slow push-ins and drifts (2–4% scale over slide duration). Cuts on beat via 12-frame luminance dissolves. Text animates in with 400 ms ease-out rise + fade; never bounces. |
| **Type** | On-screen text bilingual EN/AR. EN left-aligned LTR, AR right-aligned RTL, same weight — neither language subordinate. |
| **Palette** | Deep atmosphere blue `#0A1F3C`, gold `#BFA15A` accents, white text. (Matches STANDARD house palette.) |
| **Music** | Single evolving cue: quiet piano + low strings → full orchestral swell at Slide 8 → resolves to solo piano at Slide 10. No percussion until Slide 5. |
| **VO** | One narrator, measured diplomatic register, ~110 wpm. Record EN and AR passes; film re-times per language. |

**Campaign device:** the seven letters of **E·M·P·O·W·E·R** are revealed one per pillar slide (Slides 3–9), assembling on screen until the full word locks at Slide 9 — then resolves into the candidacy card.

## SLIDE 1 — COLD OPEN: THE SKY WE ALL SHARE
**Duration:** 0:00–0:12

- **4K visual prompt:** *Pre-dawn Earth curvature from high altitude, ultra-realistic: thin blue atmospheric band glowing over the darkened planet, city lights scattered below like embers, a slow sunrise flare cresting the horizon. Photoreal, anamorphic lens character, subtle film grain.*
- **Camera/motion:** Imperceptible drift left-to-right; sunrise intensifies across the full 12 s.
- **On-screen text (0:06):**
  - EN: `One atmosphere. One responsibility.`
  - AR: `غلاف جوي واحد. مسؤولية واحدة.`
- **VO:** "The sky belongs to no nation — and to every nation."
- **Sound:** Near-silence; a single sustained piano note fades in.
- **Transition:** Light of the sunrise blooms to white → resolves into Slide 2.

## SLIDE 2 — THE STAKES

## Production notes

1. **Slide vs. film delivery.** Each slide is built as an independent 12 s loop-safe segment (first/last frames motion-matched), so the deck can be presented manually slide-by-slide *or* exported as one continuous 2-minute film.
2. **Arabic master.** Do not mirror the EN cut mechanically: re-time text reveals RTL, re-record VO in Arabic (not subtitled), and let AR type lead on Slides 3–9. All names/titles must come from the **approved** `protocol.json` — the console you now have is the source of truth.
3. **Protocol accuracy (zero-error rule).** Slide 10's name, title, election session, and date are placeholders until verified against official WMO documentation. Nothing renders publicly before that check.
4. **Footage strategy.** Every visual prompt above works both as an AI-generation prompt (Veo/Runway-class, 4K upscale pass) and as a live-action/stock brief — shots were deliberately written to be achievable either way. For the two human-centric slides (3 and 8), prefer real footage: faces carry the campaign.
5. **Compliance check before release:** WMO campaign-communication rules on member-state election materials vary by cycle — have the mission/legal advisor confirm permitted formats and distribution channels.
