# سيناريوهات التفاعل — SCENARIO ANNEX

**Classification:** `سري — Confidential`

---

## Scenario 1 — Counterpart raises Russia LNG cooperation in restricted bilateral

- **Probability:** M
- **Read:** Japan is hedging GX-era fuel supply; may test whether KSA could be a faster alternative to risky Russia volumes.
- **Recommended response:** *"The Kingdom's energy policy is anchored to OPEC+ discipline. We are positioning to be Japan's anchor clean-fuel partner for the 2030s — that is the right frame for this conversation."*
- **Fallback:** *"Specifics on the conventional barrel are handled by the Ministry of Energy directly with METI."*
- **Hard escalation:** Defer to Ambassador post-meeting.
- **Do NOT:** characterize Russia, sanctions, or the global oil market.

## Scenario 2 — Japan side proposes joint-statement clause referencing Taiwan / Indo-Pacific

- **Read:** Likely testing for any alignment; or simply boilerplate they hope we adopt.
- **Recommended response:** *"We will need to see the proposed language in writing. Our preference is to limit the joint statement to bilateral substance and avoid third-country framings where positions diverge."*
- **Fallback:** Counter with neutral language on "rules-based international order" — pre-cleared phrasing.
- **Hard escalation:** No verbal agreement in-room; DG Asia coordinates with DG Multilateral overnight.

## Scenario 3 — Press shouts a Gaza-related question at the joint press statement

- **Recommended response:** Smile briefly, do not engage. Continue to scripted closing.
- **If pressed at stand-up:** *"Our discussions today focused on the bilateral. The Kingdom's position on regional matters is well-known."*
- **Do NOT:** make eye contact with the questioner, do not pause, do not improvise.

## Scenario 4 — Keidanren CEO asks publicly for same-day PIF commitment number

- **Recommended response:** *"Investment decisions at the scale we are discussing follow their own track at PIF. I will ensure your interest is reflected to the appropriate principals immediately upon my return — and PIF Asia is in the room today to take it forward."*
- **Why this works:** preserves the convening power without ceding capital authority.

## Scenario 5 — PM Office proposes upgraded courtesy call (e.g., adds joint media event)

- **Recommended response:** *"We would be honored. Subject to protocol confirmation by both sides."*
- **Action:** Ambassador + Chief of Staff verify pre-clearance at protocol level; check optics implications with DG Comms before agreeing.

## Scenario 6 — Protocol slip (anthem order, seating right, interpreter error)

- **Recommended response:** Ignore in the moment. Do not visibly react. Director of Protocol handles privately at break.
- **Do NOT:** correct your interpreter in the room. Note it; recalibrate after.

## Scenario 7 — Counterpart attempts a one-on-one with the Minister at the working lunch (no note-taker)

- **Recommended response:** Minister may agree to a 10–15 min private exchange. Ambassador or CoS debriefs immediately afterward. Minister writes a memcon to himself within 24 hours.
- **Do NOT:** allow longer than 15 min unsupervised; the substance must be captured.

## Scenario 8 — Major crisis breaks in the region during the visit (escalation, attack, market move)

- **Recommended response:** Brief schedule pause. Chief of Staff consults Royal Court Diwan. SPA-cleared line only.
- **Default holding line:** *"We are monitoring developments closely and remain in coordination with our partners. We will return to scheduled commitments."*
- **Do NOT:** comment publicly until Riyadh has confirmed the Kingdom's position.
- **Consider:** trimming public events; bilateral may proceed in restricted format only.

## Scenario 9 — Counterpart offers a senior-level Japanese visit to KSA (PM or Emperor visit timing)

- **Recommended response:** *"This would be deeply welcomed. We will revert through the appropriate channels."*
- **Do NOT:** commit dates or hosting protocol in-room. RCD owns this.

## Scenario 10 — Hydrogen LOI cannot be signed at the business roundtable (counterparty pulls back)

- **Recommended response:** Pivot publicly to *framework workstream commitment*. *"We have agreed clean fuels as a flagship workstream of the renewed framework. The commercial documents will follow at the working level on the agreed timeline."*
- **Internal action:** Embassy + Energy Ministry escalate post-visit; do not signal disappointment publicly.
