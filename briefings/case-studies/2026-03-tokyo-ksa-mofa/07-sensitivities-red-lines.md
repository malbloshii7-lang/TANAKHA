# الحساسيات والخطوط الحمراء — SENSITIVITIES & RED LINES

**Classification:** `سري — Confidential`
**Handling:** Minister + Chief of Staff + Ambassador only. Not for delegation-wide distribution.

---

## 1. Must NOT Say

| # | Topic | Why | If pressed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Specific timeline for Israel normalization | Position is set by RCD; any FM-level public hint moves markets and our regional standing | *"There is no change to the Kingdom's stated position."* |
| 2 | Characterization of US posture on Iran nuclear file | Risk of being read as alignment shift | *"The Kingdom engages directly with the United States on these matters."* |
| 3 | Specific public commitments on Russia LNG / oil cooperation | OPEC+ optics; sanctions implications | *"The Kingdom's policy is anchored to OPEC+ discipline."* |
| 4 | NEOM-specific revised timelines | Owned by project leadership; misstatement creates legal & investor risk | *"Sequenced for bankability; specifics with the project teams."* |
| 5 | Hydrogen LOI dollar value before signing | Not pre-cleared; counterparty will object publicly | *"We'll let the signed document speak."* |
| 6 | Comparative remarks on UAE / Qatar partnerships with Japan | Damages GCC unity narrative | *"The Kingdom's relationship with Japan stands on its own substance."* |
| 7 | Yemen specifics beyond official position | Policy is set by RCD + MoD | *"Our position on the situation in Yemen is well-established."* |
| 8 | Personal opinions on Japanese cabinet | Diplomatic norm | Smile, redirect to substance. |

## 2. Must NOT Agree To — Even Conditionally

| # | Proposal we may be offered | Reason | Decline language |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Joint statement language criticizing a third country | RCD + Royal Court sign-off required | *"Our standing position covers this; we prefer to leave the joint statement focused on bilateral substance."* |
| 2 | Same-day public PIF deployment commitment | Capital authority is PIF + RCD | *"I will reflect this to PIF Asia immediately upon my return."* |
| 3 | Defense cooperation specifics in public lines | DG Defense Affairs sign-off required | *"Defense cooperation continues at the working level."* |
| 4 | Multilateral co-sponsorship of any resolution not pre-cleared | DG Multilateral sign-off | *"We will revert through our Mission in New York."* |
| 5 | HRH-level visit timing announcement | Royal Court Diwan only | *"We look forward to continued political-level engagement."* |
| 6 | Definitive year for hydrogen offtake commercial volumes | Energy Ministry + Aramco/SABIC commercial track | *"Volumes window is the appropriate language at this stage."* |

## 3. Must NOT Be Photographed With / At

- Any opposition / non-cabinet political figure not pre-approved.
- Any cultural site whose Saudi-press optics would be problematic.
- Defense facilities — no public photo at SDF or related installations even on courtesy visit.
- Any commercial product placement.

## 4. Requires HQ Sign-off Before Any Commitment

- Arms / defense specifics → DG Defense Affairs.
- PIF deployment numbers → PIF Asia + RCD.
- Multilateral co-sponsorships → DG Multilateral.
- Public position on Iran / Yemen / Israel / Russia → RCD + Minister.
- Specific dates for HRH-level visits → RCD only.

## 5. Counterpart-Specific Sensitivities

- Avoid drawing the FM into characterizing intra-cabinet dynamics (METI vs MOFA on energy timing).
- Do not be drawn into Taiwan / China posture commentary at the press spray — host will appreciate restraint.
- Japan side is sensitive to public statements that imply ranking among Asian partners; speak of Japan in its own frame.

## 6. Internal Saudi Sensitivities

- Speak from the Kingdom's position, never personal opinion — even off-mic.
- Reference HRH the Crown Prince's vision when invoking strategic frame; do not extemporize on royal family dynamics under any circumstance.
- Vision 2030 milestones: cite only published figures.
- Religion: do not be photographed in any setting that could be misread on Saudi domestic platforms.

## 7. The "Don't Get Cute" List

- No jokes about Japanese cabinet personalities or any foreign leader.
- No off-the-record characterizations of US, China, India, or GCC peers.
- No commitments on KSA votes at multilateral forums.
- No off-cuff timelines for any megaproject.
- No personal phone numbers exchanged outside Protocol channels.
- No tweets composed by HE himself in-country without DG Comms review.

---

**If in doubt:** say less. The Minister's silence is never the story. An off-script line is.
