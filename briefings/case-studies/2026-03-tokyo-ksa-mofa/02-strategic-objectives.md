## Objective 1 — Renew the Saudi-Japan Vision 2030 Framework

- **Why this, why now:** The 2017 framework (signed under PM Abe / King Salman visit) has delivered measurable cooperation (energy, automotive, tourism) but is overdue for refresh. Japan's GX strategy and KSA's 2030 sprint create a natural alignment window now; delay risks Japanese bandwidth migrating fully to ASEAN/Taiwan.
- **Vision 2030 anchor:** Thriving Economy → PIF Program → SPARK, NIDLP industrial localization.
- **What "success" looks like:** Joint statement explicitly names a refreshed 2026–2030 work program with 5 numbered workstreams and quarterly senior-officials review.
- **Sub-objectives:**
  - Energy transition workstream (hydrogen, ammonia, LNG).
  - Investment & capital markets (PIF–GPIF/JIC/JBIC institutional dialogue).
  - Digital & AI (SDAIA – Japan AI Safety Institute; semiconductors PIF-track).
  - Tourism & people-to-people (visa liberalization expansion).
  - Multilateral coordination (G20, UN, OPEC-IEA dialogue).
- **Walk-away wins:**
  1. **PREFERRED** — signed Joint Statement with all 5 workstreams + dated review cadence.
  2. **ACCEPTABLE** — Joint Statement with 5 workstreams, review cadence "to be agreed at SOM level."
  3. **MINIMUM** — Joint Statement language committing to a refreshed framework, scope to be finalized by end-2026 SOM.

## Objective 2 — Hydrogen / Clean-Fuel Offtake LOI

- **Why this, why now:** Japan's GX program requires ~3 mtpa of imported clean ammonia by 2030; the Kingdom is positioning to be a top-3 supplier alongside Australia and UAE. UAE is moving fast (ADNOC + JERA pilots). We need a public signal that KSA is at the front of the queue.
- **Vision 2030 anchor:** Thriving Economy → Energy diversification; NEOM Green Hydrogen Project + Aramco blue ammonia.
- **What "success" looks like:** A non-binding LOI between Aramco (or SABIC) and a leading Japanese utility — JERA preferred `[VERIFY current JERA leadership posture]` — for ammonia co-firing trials, volumes window 2026–2028.
- **Sub-objectives:**
  - Named volumes (1–3 mtpa range) without firm price.
  - Joint feasibility study on receiving terminal augmentation (Onahama, Tobata).
  - METI/Energy-Ministry quarterly working group.
- **Walk-away wins:**
  1. **PREFERRED** — LOI with JERA, named volumes window, signed at the business roundtable.
  2. **ACCEPTABLE** — LOI with a second-tier utility OR an MOU between Energy Ministry and METI on hydrogen cooperation.
  3. **MINIMUM** — joint statement language naming hydrogen as a flagship workstream.

## Objective 3 — AI Cooperation MoU (SDAIA × Japan AI Safety Institute)

- **Why this, why now:** Japan's AI Safety Institute (est. 2024) is well-regarded internationally and aligned with KSA's positioning as the GCC's AI-governance lead. The MoU positions KSA as the GCC entry point for any Japan-G7 AI governance cooperation and signals seriousness on the AI ecosystem PIF is building (HUMAIN-track).
- **Vision 2030 anchor:** Ambitious Nation → Data & AI → SDAIA's national strategy; adjacent PIF Asia semiconductor positioning.
- **What "success" looks like:** Signed MoU between SDAIA and the Japan AI Safety Institute, with three named workstreams.
- **Sub-objectives:**
  - AI safety standards convergence — joint paper by end-2026.
  - Talent exchange — researcher residency program, both directions.
  - *(Stretch)* Joint compute pilot — sensitive; lead with safety + talent only.
- **Walk-away wins:**
  1. **PREFERRED** — signed MoU with all three workstreams.
  2. **ACCEPTABLE** — MoU with safety + talent; compute deferred to working level.
  3. **MINIMUM** — joint statement language identifying SDAIA – AISI cooperation as a 2026 priority.
