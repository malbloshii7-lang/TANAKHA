## 5. Lines NOT to Say

- No specific dollar figures on hydrogen volumes or LOI value.
- No characterization of Japanese cabinet politics, however favorable.
- No comparison with UAE, China, or other partners.
- No off-the-record characterization of US posture.
- No joint position on a third country without HQ sign-off.
