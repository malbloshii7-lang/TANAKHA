## Card 1 — Bilateral with HE the FM of Japan

**Date/Time/Venue:** 19 March 2026, 10:00, MOFA Iikura Annex
**Duration:** 60 min restricted + 30 min expanded
**Language:** interpreted AR↔JA, English fallback

### Opening (≤45 sec)

> **EN:** *"Excellency, thank you for hosting us. The Kingdom values the Saudi-Japan partnership as strategic and long-cycle. I come today with a clear ambition: to convert the strong sectoral cooperation of the past decade into a renewed framework with five named workstreams — and to land three concrete deliverables before the end of this visit."*
>
> **AR:** «معالي الوزير، أشكركم على حسن الاستقبال. تُولي المملكة الشراكة السعودية–اليابانية قيمة استراتيجية وطويلة المدى. أحضر اليوم بطموح واضح: تحويل التعاون القطاعي القوي للعقد الماضي إلى إطار مُجدَّد ذي خمسة محاور محددة، وتحقيق ثلاث نتائج ملموسة قبل ختام هذه الزيارة.»

### Three Points (in order)

**Point 1 — Framework Renewal.**
- One-line: *Renew the 2017 framework into a 2026–2030 work program with 5 numbered workstreams and a quarterly SOM review.*
- Support: 9 years of delivery — energy, automotive, tourism, education — earn the next chapter.
- The ask hidden: joint statement explicit naming of workstreams + SOM cadence.
- AR delivery: «نقترح تجديد إطار 2017 ببرنامج عمل 2026–2030 يتضمن خمسة محاور وآلية مراجعة فصلية.»

**Point 2 — Hydrogen / Clean-Fuel Offtake.**
- One-line: *The Kingdom is positioning to be Japan's anchor clean-fuel supplier for the 2030s; we'd like a signal from JERA-tier this visit.*
- Support: Aramco / SABIC blue+green ammonia program; NEOM Green H2; Japan's GX requires ~3 mtpa imports by 2030.
- Ask: LOI with named volumes window 1–3 mtpa by 2028, signed at the business roundtable.
- AR delivery: «نضع المملكة في موقع المورّد المرجعي للوقود النظيف لليابان للعقد القادم، ونتطلع لإشارة من شركة كبرى خلال هذه الزيارة.»

**Point 3 — AI Cooperation (SDAIA × Japan AISI).**
- One-line: *Japan's AI Safety Institute and SDAIA are natural partners on standards, talent, and the next governance cycle.*
- Support: KSA's positioning as the GCC AI-governance lead; PIF's adjacent semiconductor investments; HUMAIN.
- Ask: MoU signed at sidebar; three workstreams (safety, talent, joint compute as stretch).
- AR delivery: «معهد سلامة الذكاء الاصطناعي الياباني وهيئة سدايا شريكان طبيعيان في المعايير وتبادل الكفاءات.»

### The Ask

> *"Excellency, I would like to leave this room having committed to (a) the five-workstream framework renewal, (b) hydrogen as a named flagship workstream, and (c) the SDAIA – AISI MoU. All three are within my mandate today."*

**Acceptable counter-offers:**
1. All three with review-cadence to be agreed at SOM.
2. Two of three, with the third deferred to end-2026.

**Walk-away line:** *"We will return to this at the September UNGA margin; in the meantime our teams will continue at the technical level."*

### Close

> **EN:** *"Excellency, thank you for an exchange that reflects the seriousness of our partnership. I look forward to seeing the substance reflected in our joint statement."*
>
> **AR:** «معالي الوزير، أشكركم على حوار يعكس جدية شراكتنا. وأتطلع لرؤية مضمونه في بياننا المشترك.»

### "If Pressed" Defensive Lines

| If raised | We say |
|---|---|
| Russia LNG / oil cooperation | *"The Kingdom's energy policy is anchored to OPEC+ discipline. Specifics belong at the working level."* |
| Iran nuclear file | *"We support diplomacy that delivers verifiable nonproliferation. Our specific concerns are well-known to our partners."* |
| Israel normalization | *"There is no change to the Kingdom's stated position."* |
| NEOM project timelines | *"We are sequencing for bankability. Happy to host a JBIC/METI delegation Q3."* |
| Public PIF deployment commitment | *"Investment decisions of that scale follow their own track at PIF. Your interest will be reflected immediately upon my return."* |

### Avoid

- Specific dollar figures on hydrogen.
- Any timeline on Israel normalization.
- Characterizing US or China posture in the region.
- Public position on Russia.

### Post-Meeting

- [ ] Memcon by DG Asia within 24h.
- [ ] Joint statement to be finalized at expanded session 11:00–11:30.
- [ ] SPA readout coordinated with MOFA spokesperson per Section 06.

## Card 4 — Keidanren Business Roundtable

**Date/Time/Venue:** 20 March 2026, 10:00, Keidanren Hall
**Format:** Keynote + roundtable, ~40 attendees
**Duration:** 90 min total (20 min keynote + 70 min Q&A)

### Keynote spine (20 min)

> *Predictable. Profitable. Patient capital welcomed.*

- 5 min: Vision 2030 progress in numbers — GDP composition, non-oil PMI, tourism arrivals, female labor participation.
- 5 min: anchor projects open for Japanese capital — SPARK, NIDLP industrial cities, tourism mega-projects, PIF co-investment vehicles.
- 5 min: regulatory rails — Investment Treaty close to finalization, dispute resolution, capital mobility.
- 5 min: invitation — "Be our anchor industrial partner for the second arc."

### Q&A defensive lines

| Q | Response |
|---|---|
| NEOM timeline | *"Sequenced for bankability; phased delivery; specifics with the project teams."* |
| Currency / capital controls | *"Riyal peg is stable. Capital mobility is high. Our regulators are accessible."* |
| Workforce localization | *"Saudization works through partnership, not friction; we have flexibility on senior expatriate roles."* |
| Geopolitics | *"The Kingdom is a stabilizer. Our regional posture serves long-term investor confidence."* |

### Avoid

- Specific deal numbers.
- Public commitment of PIF capital.
- Comparisons with UAE or other GCC peers.
- Off-cuff timelines for any megaproject.

- Embassy hosts side-meeting with 5 priority Japanese CEOs immediately after.
- LOI signings (if landed) at coffee break.
- Speech text released to wires under SPA-coordinated embargo.
