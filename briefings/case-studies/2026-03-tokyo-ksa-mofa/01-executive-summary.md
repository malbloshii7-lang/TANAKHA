## أهم ثلاثة أهداف / Top 3 Objectives

1. **Renew the Saudi-Japan Vision 2030 framework** (originally 2017) with a refreshed 2026–2030 work program. **Anchor:** Vision 2030 → Thriving Economy pillar → King Salman Energy Park (SPARK) and NIDLP. **KPI:** signed renewal communiqué naming five workstreams.
2. **Hydrogen / clean-fuel offtake.** Secure a Letter of Intent from a leading Japanese power utility (`[VERIFY: JERA preferred per latest read]`) on Saudi blue/green ammonia co-firing trials at thermal sites, 2026–2028. **Anchor:** Vibrant Economy → Energy diversification; aligned with Japan's GX (Green Transformation) strategy. **KPI:** non-binding LOI with named volumes window.
3. **AI cooperation MoU.** Frame KSA's Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) and the Japan AI Safety Institute as natural counterparts on safety standards, talent exchange, and joint compute pilots. **Anchor:** Ambitious Nation → digital infrastructure; PIF-track via investment in Japan's semiconductor ecosystem (Rapidus / TSMC Kumamoto adjacency). **KPI:** signed MoU with workstreams + named technical leads.
