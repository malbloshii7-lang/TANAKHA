## What This Visit Is About — In One Paragraph

Japan is the Kingdom's highest-quality long-cycle partner in Asia for the energy transition and institutional capital, but Tokyo's foreign-policy bandwidth has drifted to US, Taiwan, and ASEAN files. This visit renews the 2017 Saudi-Japan Vision 2030 framework with five named workstreams, anchors a hydrogen / clean-fuel LOI with a leading Japanese utility, and signs an SDAIA × Japan AI Safety Institute MoU. If we land these three, the visit reads as substantive; if we land only the framework renewal, it reads as ceremonial in a year when ceremony is not enough.

## Production Note

A real production pack typically runs 3–5x the length of this case study, with: (a) per-counterpart bio in a separate file with embedded photo, (b) per-meeting talking-points card in a separate file, (c) full per-day minute-by-minute schedule pocket card, (d) embassy on-ground intelligence as a separate annex, (e) sectoral technical annexes (energy, AI, investment) drafted by sector ministries and bound in.

This case study is sufficient to:
- Demonstrate the canonical structure end-to-end.
- Train an orchestrating agent on what each section should contain.
- Serve as a teaching artifact for new desk officers.

It is **not** sufficient to:
- Brief a real Minister for a real visit. Real packs require ground-truth on every `[VERIFY]` item, embassy on-ground intelligence, and inter-agency clearance per the sign-off chain in Section 00.
