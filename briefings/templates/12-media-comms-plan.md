# خطة الإعلام والاتصال — MEDIA & COMMUNICATIONS PLAN

**Classification:** `محدود — Restricted`

---

## 1. Strategic Objective for Visibility

> ONE_LINE — what we want the Saudi public, host public, and regional/global audiences to take away. This is the test for every comms decision.

## 2. Audiences & Channels

| Audience | Primary channel | Key message | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saudi domestic | SPA, Al Ekhbariya, Asharq | "Vision 2030 partnership delivering" | MoFA Comms |
| Host country | Host wires + national broadcaster | "Long-cycle commitment" | Embassy + DG Comms |
| Regional (GCC + MENA) | Asharq, Al Arabiya, regional wires | "Coordinated Gulf posture" | DG Comms |
| Global / business | Reuters, FT, Bloomberg, Nikkei (if Japan) | "Investable, predictable, ambitious" | DG Comms + Embassy |
| Specialist (energy/tech) | Trade press | Sector-specific | Sector ministry counterparts |

## 3. Pre-Visit (T-7 to T-0)

- T-7: Embassy briefs host political correspondents on background — no quotes from Minister.
- T-5: SPA pre-announces visit dates + headline themes (no specifics).
- T-3: Op-ed by HE the Minister placed in host's leading paper (drafted T-10, cleared T-5).
- T-1: Wheels-up note from KSA Embassy social channels.

## 4. During Visit

| Touchpoint | Format | Owner | Approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tarmac arrival photo | SPA pool + embassy social | Embassy | DG Comms |
| Pre-bilateral spray | Pool, no Q&A | Host protocol + KSA Comms | DG Comms |
| Joint press statement | Standing, scripted | Both spokespersons | Minister cleared |
| Post-bilateral readout | SPA wire | DG Comms | Chief of Staff cleared |
| Business roundtable speech | Full text release | DG Comms + sector ministry | Chief of Staff cleared |
| Social media throughout | X, Instagram | Digital officer | Spokesperson cleared |

## 5. Post-Visit (T+1 to T+7)

- T+1: Wheels-down readout cable; SPA closing wire.
- T+2: Op-ed by HE the Minister in Asharq Al-Awsat reflecting on outcomes.
- T+3: Embassy hosts host-country journalist roundtable in HOST_CAPITAL (background).
- T+7: Outcomes tracker shared internally; one-page public summary of deliverables.

## 6. Social Media Pre-Cleared Posts

*Stored in Section 06. Digital officer schedules with embargoes per host comms coordination.*

## 7. Crisis Comms — If the Visit Is Overtaken by Events

- Default holding line: "We are focused on the bilateral; we are monitoring developments closely."
- Decision tree for escalation:
  1. News breaks → Chief of Staff alerted within 5 min.
  2. CoS + Ambassador consult Royal Court Diwan if regional/political.
  3. SPA-cleared line issued within 30 min if comment required.
  4. No off-the-record briefings until cleared.

## 8. Visibility KPIs (for post-visit memo)

- Number of pickups in host top-10 outlets.
- Reach of joint statement in Saudi press.
- Engagement on KSA-side social posts.
- Tone analysis (positive / neutral / negative) in host coverage.
- Number of follow-up requests from journalists for background.
