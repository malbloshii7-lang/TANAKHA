# التصريحات الصحفية والأسئلة والأجوبة — PRESS LINES & Q&A

**Classification:** `سري — Confidential` (lines marked `[PUBLIC]` are pre-cleared for use.)

---

## 1. Core Public Narrative (bilingual)

> **EN [PUBLIC]:** "This visit reflects the strength and maturity of the partnership between the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and HOST. Both sides are committed to translating shared ambitions — under Vision 2030 and HOST's national strategy — into tangible outcomes for our peoples."
>
> **AR [PUBLIC]:** «تعكس هذه الزيارة قوة ونضج الشراكة بين المملكة العربية السعودية و**HOST**. ويلتزم الجانبان بترجمة طموحاتنا المشتركة — في إطار رؤية 2030 والاستراتيجية الوطنية لـ**HOST** — إلى نتائج ملموسة لشعوبنا.»

## 2. SPA-Ready Readout (Arabic, formal register)

> **AR [PUBLIC]:** «استقبل معالي وزير خارجية **HOST** سعادة... وجرى خلال اللقاء بحث العلاقات الثنائية وسبل تعزيزها في مختلف المجالات، إضافة إلى التطورات الإقليمية والدولية ذات الاهتمام المشترك.»

*Tighten verbs once meetings concluded. Avoid concrete numbers until both sides sign off.*

## 3. Approved Talking Points for Stand-Up / Pool Spray

- "[PUBLIC] We had a constructive and substantive discussion focused on bilateral cooperation and the region."
- "[PUBLIC] The Kingdom and HOST share a clear vision of partnership for the long term."
- "[PUBLIC] Specific outcomes will be reflected in our joint statement."

## 4. Hostile Q&A Lines

| Q | Recommended response |
|---|---|
| "Did you discuss FILE_X?" | "Our discussions covered the full range of bilateral matters; the joint statement will reflect what was agreed." |
| "What is KSA's position on COUNTRY_Y?" | "The Kingdom's position on COUNTRY_Y is well-established. Today's visit is focused on our bilateral with HOST." |
| "Did you raise human rights concerns?" | "The Kingdom engages with all partners on the full agenda, and reform under Vision 2030 has delivered measurable progress." |
| "Was there discussion of arms / defense?" | "Defense and security cooperation is a long-standing element of the partnership; specifics are handled at the working level." |
| "Is normalization with COUNTRY_Z on the table?" | "There is no change to the Kingdom's stated position." |
| Surprise / off-script | "I'll revert through our Embassy. Thank you." Walk on. |

## 5. Lines NOT to Say

- Avoid specific dollar figures unless both sides have signed off.
- Avoid characterizing the host's domestic politics, even positively.
- Avoid joint position on a third country without HQ sign-off.
- Avoid jokes that don't translate. (When in doubt, don't.)

## 6. Social Media Pre-Approved Posts

**X / Twitter (Arabic, ≤280 chars):**
> «بحثت اليوم مع معالي وزير خارجية **HOST**... سبل تعزيز الشراكة الاستراتيجية بين بلدينا في إطار رؤية 2030.»

**X / Twitter (English, ≤280 chars):**
> "Constructive meeting today with HE NAME of HOST. Reaffirmed our commitment to a long-term strategic partnership under Vision 2030."

**Photo brief for digital team:** 3 hero shots — handshake, bilateral table, signing (if applicable). Anthem order and flag positioning per Section 09.

## 7. Embargo & Coordination

- **Embargo on joint statement:** until HH:MM local, coordinated with host comms.
- **SPA release sequencing:** announce → readout → joint statement → minister tweet.
- **No background briefings off the record unless cleared by DG Comms.**
