## 3. Cultural Do's

- Greet using local greeting + handshake (firm, brief). If host is non-mahram woman in conservative-influence context, follow her lead — verbal greeting suffices.
- Use the host's exact title and full name on first reference; "Excellency" thereafter.
- Allow the host to lead seating, opening remarks, and meal initiation.
- Acknowledge host's national symbols, history, and recent achievements in opening remarks.

## 4. Cultural Don'ts

- Do not point with the index finger, especially during photo ops; use open hand.
- Do not show the sole of the shoe in seated positions.
- Do not gesture with the left hand for receiving or giving.
- Do not photograph during prayer; do not interrupt during host's prayer/ritual.
- Do not joke about religion, royalty, gender, or domestic politics — even self-deprecating.
- Do not be the first to leave a meal unless schedule dictates and a graceful exit line is pre-prepared.
