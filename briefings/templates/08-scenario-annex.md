# سيناريوهات التفاعل — SCENARIO ANNEX

**Classification:** `سري — Confidential`

> *Five to eight curveballs the Minister may face in-room. For each: probability, recommended response, fallback, escalation path.*

---

## Scenario 1 — Counterpart raises `SENSITIVE_ISSUE` unexpectedly

- **Probability:** L / M / H
- **Read:** WHY they would raise it now.
- **Recommended response:** ONE_LINE primary, ONE_LINE bridge to our agenda.
- **Fallback if pressed twice:** ONE_LINE.
- **Hard escalation:** "I would prefer to address this through our Ambassadors after the visit."
- **Do NOT:** make any new commitment, even hedged.

## Scenario 2 — Counterpart proposes a joint statement clause we have not pre-cleared

- **Read:** are they testing us, or did their drafters add it late?
- **Recommended response:** "We will need to see the proposed language in writing. Our preference is to limit the joint statement to areas where we have alignment at the technical level."
- **Fallback:** offer to defer the clause to a follow-up communique within 30 days.
- **Hard escalation:** Minister consults Chief of Staff before agreeing. No verbal sign-off in-room.

## Scenario 3 — Press shouts a question on `THIRD_COUNTRY_FILE` during the spray

- **Recommended response:** smile, do not engage substantively. "Today we are focused on our bilateral with HOST."
- **If repeated:** brief nod, move on.
- **Do NOT:** make eye contact with the questioner; that signals you may answer.

## Scenario 4 — Counterpart asks for a same-day commitment on PIF / Aramco / SABIC investment specifics

- **Recommended response:** "Investment decisions on the scale we are discussing follow their own track at PIF / Aramco. I will ensure your interest is reflected to the appropriate principals immediately upon my return."
- **Why this is the line:** never commit on capital you do not control. The Minister carries the convening power, not the cheque book.

## Scenario 5 — Host side proposes an unscheduled higher-level engagement (PM / HoS courtesy call)

- **Recommended response:** "We would be honored. Subject to the protocol arrangements being confirmed by both sides."
- **Action:** Ambassador + Chief of Staff verify the courtesy call has been pre-cleared at the appropriate level; do not appear surprised.

## Scenario 6 — Protocol slip (seating / flag / anthem / interpreter error)

- **Recommended response:** ignore in the moment. Do not visibly react. Protocol officer handles privately at the break.
- **Do NOT:** correct your interpreter in the room. Note it and recalibrate after.

## Scenario 7 — Counterpart attempts a private one-on-one with the Minister mid-visit (no note-taker, no ambassador)

- **Recommended response:** the Minister may agree to a short private exchange. The Ambassador or Chief of Staff debriefs immediately afterward. A memcon is written by the Minister to himself within 24 hours.
- **Do NOT:** allow a private exchange longer than 15 minutes without a clear agenda.

## Scenario 8 — Crisis news breaks in the region mid-visit

- **Recommended response:** brief pause; consult Chief of Staff + Royal Court Diwan; SPA-cleared line only.
- **Default holding line:** "We are monitoring developments closely and remain in coordination with our partners."
- **Do NOT:** comment publicly until the Kingdom's position has been confirmed by Riyadh.
